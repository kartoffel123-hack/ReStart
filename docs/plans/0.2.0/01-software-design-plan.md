# Software Design Plan

**ReStart 0.2.0 · Replace the helicopter demo with the LEGO CT scanner (4000041)**

A mechanic opens the same hosted URL, picks one particular piece in the CT's MRI or Mid section, and uses the existing **Mechanic Manual** to exchange it and put the model back into service. For this demo, include only the Fusion component groups named `MRI` and `Mid section`; exclude `Base`, `Back room`, `Control room`, `Decoration`, and `Minifigs` from the Fusion demo assembly and browser scene. Also exclude the flexible string/rope component from the demo assembly and browser inventory, even if nested under an included group. Keep the complete source archive immutable and retain its reconciliation report; excluded source placements are not demo inventory or repair targets. Aim for five demonstrable single-piece repairs; the minimum for switching the demo is **one complete CT repair**, independently replayed, completed in the production browser, and demonstrated uncoached on the kit. Until there is a kit trial, report software readiness separately. No CT repair is verified at plan handoff.

## Repair target selection

The lead candidate is **the black 60-tooth turntable top inside the MRI**, `4000041/L112`, at source origin `(859.3325, −141.9997, 180.0021)` LDU. The existing STEP crosswalk places it at `MRI:1/88738_raw.dat:1`; `4000041/L192` (`18939.dat`) is its separate light-grey bottom. The [scope record](../../../data/review/4000041/demo-assembly.json) identifies both and excludes the string `4000041/L111`, leaving 284 demo placements. Confirm those identities in the new scoped export. This is a candidate, not yet a verified repair. Establish why its replacement is harder than the current gantry-support route through measured access, blockers, tool envelope, and the complete return path.

First perform a read-only candidate survey of the MRI gear and confirm the physical spare and interface. If its route cannot be validated, retain the gantry route as a fallback only if it belongs to the selected scope; otherwise choose another MRI/Mid-section occurrence. Record rejected candidates and reasons. Freeze one successful route before expanding coverage. Report counts against the scoped inventory and state the full-source reconciliation denominator separately.

## Product and data decisions

- **One product, two model packages.** `#/`, `#/review`, `#/graph` and `#/dof` follow one active dataset. Preserve the dev concept gallery and the helicopter package as a regression fixture and rollback destination. No parallel CT-only manual screen. Keep joint repair available where a verified joint route exists; an assembly with one single-target route does not need a fictitious joint route.
- **Identity survives conversion.** A revision-bound source placement in the selected MRI/Mid-section scope maps to a Fusion occurrence, STEP placement, installed transform, graph occurrence, inventory row and pickable GLB node. Reuse geometry for repeated definitions; selections, colours and motions belong to occurrences. A multi-body piece still moves as one piece. Document any composite with independently serviceable children and count its physical pieces only once. Retain explicit exclusion records for other source groups; never silently present excluded placements as missing geometry inside the scoped demo.
- **Mechanic Manual is the contract.** Search or raycast to the same instance, show an unambiguous overview and part inset, name the tool, approach side and stop landmark. Animate reviewed access, removal, exchange, insertion and restoration. Only confirmation advances physical state. Preserve inspection/history, replay, orbit/reset, checkpoints, reduced motion, fit-check failure/recheck, completion and export. Use scanner/door/table landmarks from the actual model in place of helicopter camera cues. An unsupported piece remains selectable with an explicit reason and no start button.
- **Minimum disassembly retains its meaning.** Count distinct healthy occurrences disturbed over the complete job, including module members and return-only moves. Keep containment, connection and directional requirements separate. Display the actual planner status and coverage. Kit fit/swing checks concern the LEGO assembly only. Use measured timing or reviewed, clearly labelled fictitious timing proxies; unknowns stay unknown. Helicopter workbook material, timing, contacts and bench records do not become CT facts.
- **Publication boundary.** Fusion and Studio are authoring tools. Ship only a checked static package; the mechanic needs neither installation nor sign-in. Load validated assets of one revision or offer retry. A failed CT fetch must never silently open a helicopter procedure. The [acceptance plan](03-software-verification-plan.md) distinguishes CT demo readiness from the existing 70-target helicopter release policy.
