Appendix A1 · Video 1 · Intro (slide 1)

What it shows. A snowy alpine slope right after an avalanche. A rescuer kneels next to a probe pole and waves for help. Cut to the helipad: a red rescue helicopter, crew runs to it, the pilot flips switches, a warning light flashes, the rotor stays still.

Tool. Nano Banana is Google's image model. For video use Veo (Gemini or Google Flow). Two 8 s clips are easier than one long clip; keep the same style words.

Cinematic 8-second shot, photorealistic, bright winter daylight. A steep snowy alpine slope just after an avalanche, powder snow still drifting in the air, clear blue sky. A mountain rescuer in a red jacket kneels next to an avalanche probe pole stuck in the snow and waves urgently towards the valley. Handheld camera, shallow depth of field, tense mood, wind noise, no music, no text on screen. Second shot, same style: a red-and-white mountain rescue helicopter on a snowy helipad. Two crew members in flight suits run towards it. The pilot flips switches in the cockpit, an amber warning light flashes on the instrument panel, the rotor blades stay still. Close-up of the pilot's worried face. Photorealistic, 16:9, no logos, no readable text.
Appendix A2 · Video 2 · Rescue and transition to the real world (slide 6)

What it shows. The repaired LEGO helicopter lifts off from a snowy LEGO landscape and flies a quick rescue: hovers, a LEGO rescuer is lowered, the buried LEGO figure is pulled out. The camera keeps pulling back; the LEGO scene turns into a real rescue helicopter flying over real alpine peaks.

How to make it. Create a start frame (LEGO scene) and an end frame (real helicopter) with Nano Banana, then use Veo "frames to video" for the transition. The exploded-view clip for slide 4 is best recorded from our CAD model.

Cinematic 8-second continuous shot, 16:9, bright sunny winter light. A small toy rescue helicopter built from black, light-grey and lime-green technical construction parts lifts off from a snowy toy-brick mountain landscape, flies over an avalanche field and hovers; a small toy rescuer is lowered on a winch and pulls a buried toy figure out of the snow. Without a cut, the camera pulls back and rises smoothly, and the toy scene seamlessly transforms into a real full-size red-and-white mountain rescue helicopter flying over sunlit snowy alpine peaks. End on a wide aerial shot. Hopeful, uplifting mood, photorealistic in the end, no text, no logos.
