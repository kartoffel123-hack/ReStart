Hospital. About 1.8 % of operating time is lost to unplanned downtime (87.5 of 4,700 h). Across Germany that is roughly 240,000 CT exams a year that are delayed or sent elsewhere (estimate).

Manufacturer. Service is 40–60 % of a vendor's revenue (DOTmed 2015). Every hour below the uptime guarantee costs the vendor free contract weeks. Faster repairs protect that margin.
